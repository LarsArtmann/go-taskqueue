## d) TOTALLY FUCKED UP (this window) — caught, lessons

1. **Behavioral bug in my own complexity-extraction**: the first
   `repoState.observe` draft had unreachable switch arms
   (`case Pending, Running:` swallowing the later `case Running:` /
   `case Pending:` arms) — `anyRunning` and `pendingCount` would have
   silently stayed 0, breaking the occupancy rules — and the caller
   rewrite dropped `hasDead` entirely (poison protection lost). Caught
   by re-reading the diff before testing, fixed before anything ran.
   Lesson: extraction refactors must re-derive the DECISION TABLE, not
   just move lines.
2. **Unconditional wrap broke success**: `SavePriorityScore` fix wrapped
   `err` unconditionally — `fmt.Errorf` of nil = `"%!w(<nil>)"`, caught
   by the live-postgres conformance suite. The conditional form is the
   only correct one; "wrap the return" is not mechanical.
3. **Silent assertion drop in a test refactor**: extracting the
   fact-count helper in `unblock_test.go` accidentally deleted the
   `mustPriority(hot, 120)` hot-protection assertion. Caught on diff
   re-read, restored.
4. **Slip shipped to disk**: `ctxOf(t)` — a helper that doesn't exist —
   in `show_test.go` (caught by build); an identical old/new no-op edit;
   a dead `tracked()` helper left after simplification. All trivial,
   all evidence that compile + re-read are non-negotiable.
5. **Lint whack-a-mole**: fixing growth surfaced four rounds of
   second-order findings (my new switches → exhaustive; if-chains →
   staticcheck QF1003; the resolution is the explicit-no-op-case arm;
   makezero `always:true` flags variable-LENGTH makes — the clean form
   is `make(0, cap)` + append, opposite of my first instinct). The
   classifier could not see complexity growth attributed to old lines
   (pruneRepo) — count math from the GATE, not the classifier, is
   ground truth.
6. **Adopted a concurrent change's direction before verifying it**: my
   first go.mod resolution aligned everything DOWN to `go 1.26`
   (honoring the 15:27 lowering + adding a root `toolchain` line) —
   `go mod tidy` immediately reverted it, exposing the dependency
   floor. When a gate fails on a concurrent agent's change, derive the
   correct direction from the tooling's own enforcement (tidy, the
   gate script) before building on either side.
7. **The prior window's templ indentation debt reached this window's
   gate**: 16-28 §d4 shipped four guessed-indentation attempts; the
