   three `TestDoctorServiceContext*` tests all swap `doctorReadServiceUnit`

   parallel. A cleanup can restore the real `systemctl` reader before another

   checkPhase (`-covermode=atomic` + build-time CPU contention) widened it, and the
   sandbox has no systemctl — producing exactly the five observed failures.
   Fix: dropped `t.Parallel()` from the three (they're microsecond-scale tests;
   tests that mutate package globals must not be parallel). NOTE: the concurrent
