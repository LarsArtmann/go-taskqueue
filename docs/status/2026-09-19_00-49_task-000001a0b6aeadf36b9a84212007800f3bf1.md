   chain began with `git status --short` — which printed CLEAN while I
   knew I had just edited three files. That clean status WAS the
   auto-commit daemon's signature (it had folded my files into heuristic
   commit 7c0d8b4 during the gate runs), and the `&&` chain barreled
   straight into `git add` (no-op) + `git commit` ("nothing to commit",

   daemon commit was unpushed, its `--stat` showed exactly my 3 files and
   nothing else, so I amended it into the real commit (7c0d8b4). But the
   failure mode — a blind `&&` chain marching past a contradicting signal
   — is the exact class the AGENTS.md pipeline-masking lesson warns about.
   The correct behavior: clean status immediately after your own edits ⇒
   STOP the chain, `git log` first, then choose amend vs follow-up.
