   entanglement) burned iterations on in-place logging before

   near-deletion is the text-editing twin of the 02-56 §d1 regex miss:
   the tool did what it was told; the instruction's blast radius was
   unverified. Habit to install: after any multi-row TODO edit, grep
   for the rows ADJACENT to the edit span, not just the new text.

   after item 1's "done"; fixing them re-touched three committed files

   AGENTS documents the clean-before-regen rule and it mattered).

   (slog via a var the gate can no-op) or scratch _test.go convention
