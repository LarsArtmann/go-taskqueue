  pass) so the daemon's commits land INSIDE the attributed lineage; amend
  nothing afterwards — follow-ups ride as their own footered commits.

  you moved, even when the unit suite covers it — the smoke is the
  end-to-end citation.

  now twice-ignored — promote it from lesson to habit or a script).

  command; the session-close bridge section is the canonical place.
- multiedit old_strings must never span structural boundaries (helper +
  following symbol) — use lsp_replace_symbol or smaller anchors.
